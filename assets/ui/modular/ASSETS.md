# Modular HUD assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each independent widget owns its child content; the full-screen HUD PNG is no longer referenced.

| avatar-badge/digits/*.png and *-alpha.png | Individually cropped digit sprites and single-channel alpha masks | 128 × 128 |

All live numerals sample individual sprite cells. Letters use the SDK's native serif font. Packaging only downsamples/crops the generated sources and preserves alpha; scripts/package-hud.ps1 records source IDs. No full-screen particles, fake multipliers, or baked meter values.

At 30 seconds of earned airtime, a 0.55-second wind-flash plays without a fixed banner. At 60 seconds and each minute after, the golden vortex sprite sheet loops for 2.4 seconds. Every 100 seconds through 1,000 seconds, one of ten generated winged award frames appears with exact SDK text and briefly celebrates at screen center before fading; the avatar badge itself advances to the corresponding tier. The frame uses the highest tier after 1,000 seconds. Paused time does not advance effects; replay resets the attempt's milestone tier. The ten frames are individually generated from the supplied gold-winged ruby plaque reference and packaged at 640 × 214; run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/package-milestones.ps1` to repack them. All celebration art is transparent and adds about 2 MB compressed. Each runner's whole-second airtime is displayed above their head on a synced billboard, using a red winged plate and individually cropped gold digit sprites. Unlit materials use explicit single-channel alpha textures generated by `node scripts/generate-avatar-badge-textures.cjs`; this preserves transparent cutouts. Each client creates and synchronizes its local avatar's head anchor, billboard, plate, digits and material/visibility updates so nearby players can see the badge. The badge sits 1 m above the head anchor and the plate advances every 100 seconds. At every 30-second airtime mark, a rotating vortex sprite plays above the plate for 2.4 seconds; its alpha, visibility and animated transform are synchronized for nearby players. The fixed blue timer shows seconds, best time, concise phase status and local orb progress inside a matching row-style plaque; the fixed red timer plate is removed. The timer, nearby list, details and altitude cards share one width; the altitude gauge sits bottom-center. Redundant run-state and instruction copy is omitted. Persistent global standings live on a physical world-space board by a concrete pillar. The left side, main playfield and lower corners stay clear. Portrait canvases keep the compact timer and orb counter. One cached UI tree updates at most 20 times per second. Desktop shows five leaders and compact landscape shows three. Visual checks use a browser component fixture; native mobile FPS and final Decentraland client rendering still need a device check.
