# Endless Vortex — airtime prototype

Stay airborne in the enlarged wind tunnel. The build occupies **3x3 parcels (48x48 metres)**, centered at x/z 24. The model, height, wind volumes and floor spawn are uniformly scaled 1.5x from the 2x2 prototype. The central air tunnel lift and tangential vortex rotation activate from 1.8 metres upward; inward/outer containment pull, vortex lift and the broad updraft remain paused. Native steering, jumping, gliding, orbiting leaf platforms and golden recovery orbs remain active. Wind-plane effects remain removed, and the original looping theme music is active again.

## Active behavior

- The `endless_vortex6.glb` environment is authored in the scene at (23.75, 0, 24.25), scale 1.4. Its visible meshes provide physics and pointer collision; invisible helper meshes are non-colliding. Runtime uses this environment in place of the old wind-tunnel model. Airtime boundaries remain radius 21.6 m and height 30 m.
- `AIRFLOW.centralOnly = true` temporarily enables only the central vertical wind volume: **32 force on desktop / 14 on mobile**. It is centered at x/z 24, spans 5.4 m across each horizontal axis and reaches y 24.375 m. Outside this volume, no continuous wind force is applied. Bubble contact can still give a small lift.
- Active vortex rotation uses spin 11. Saved inward pull 1.05, outer containment 2.94 and vortex lift 2.25 remain available. Set `centralOnly` to `false` in `src/vortex.ts` and rebuild to restore these and the broad updraft. Desktop/mobile multipliers remain 0.8/0.35; restoring broad lift would add 8/3.5 respectively. Pausing player wind does not pause platform orbiting.
- The 48 decorative wind planes (ribbons and segmented rings), their entities and animation system remain absent; `src/vortex-visuals.ts` is dormant.
- Normal client movement, jumping and gliding. No locomotion overrides or disabled glider.
- Original ground-level spawn. Airflow works immediately, without an attempt/start condition.

- Airtime is the result. Takeoff starts counting automatically; platform landing pauses it; jumping or stepping off resumes it. Bottom-floor contact finishes and saves the run. The next takeoff retries without clearing the best.
- Standing adds no time. Leaving the playable arena pauses the run; returning resumes it when airborne, and the outside/re-entry intervals do not count. Touching the floor still ends the attempt. An unexpected teleport, lost connection after connecting, or sample gap over 0.5 seconds finishes the observed run; land before retrying after those interruptions. Desktop pointer unlock pauses counting; time never catches up after suspension.
- Up to **eighteen leaf platforms** appear randomly at 24 allowed positions, at heights 5.25, 9 and 12.75 metres. The first appears after 0.6 seconds, with further spawn opportunities every 0.7–1.05 seconds until the pool is full. Each uses the supplied `leaf1.glb` at uniform scale and its natural 3.86 × 1.96 m footprint, with a centered pivot and matching elliptical landing sensor. Leaves orbit at **8 degrees/second** near a **14.5 m radius**, 2.5 m closer to the center than before, spin independently at 35–70 degrees/second and tumble through a restrained 40-degree range.
- The visible leaf GLB mesh is its physical collider; there is no separate disk or oversized invisible collider. Landing checks follow the same tilted top plane. Leaves are fully opaque with no overhead labels. Player clearance is checked at appearance and activation, and untouched leaves remain available without age-based expiry.
- The first landing starts a **2-second countdown**. It keeps counting after departure; repeated landings never reset it. A quick scale pulse warns throughout the countdown without transparency or labels. At zero, collision is removed and the opaque leaf visibly drops, then is hidden at floor height or after 2 seconds. Falling visuals cannot push a player or act as another landing surface. Unused leaves remain unaffected; replacements reuse the same pool.
- Landing contact runs every frame, independently of the 20 Hz airtime samples. Each solid leaf has a non-solid native `TriggerArea` sensor immediately above its rotated top plane. Only local-player ENTER/EXIT events affect it. Native and geometric checks reject overhead passes and underside contact. The fixed eighteen sensors and thirty-six listeners are reused and cleared on collapse, hiding or suspension.
- The ground ray and geometric checks remain fallbacks for clients without trigger events. A confirmed ground ray arms the specific platform it hits; geometry checks current and previous frame positions, with a 0.22 m vertical tolerance. A stale ray onto an already collapsed platform cannot keep it supporting the player. Native sensor delivery and tolerances still require desktop/mobile gameplay verification. See the [SDK trigger-area documentation](https://docs.decentraland.org/creator/scenes-sdk7/3d-content-essentials/trigger-areas).
- Visuals, colliders and support detection follow the same wind-wobbled orbit until collapse; falling visuals drop vertically. Position updates run every frame; warning styles update at 10 Hz. The fixed entity pool remains local to each player. A frame gap over 0.5 seconds clears platforms before restarting the spawn cycle, avoiding a large collider sweep on resume. Wind pauses on supported elevated surfaces. Native clients handle player contact; the only scripted teleport is the local ten-orb reward described below.
- The HUD is designed for desktop and landscape phone canvases only; it renders no HUD in portrait orientation. The live airtime plaque sits top-right, the winged streak banner and lift/sound buttons sit top-center, and a nearby-scene-player airtime list, run details and compact teleport-orb counter sit mid-right. A single simple altitude gauge sits bottom-center. The left side, main playfield and lower corners stay clear. Persistent weekly global standings are shown on a physical board mounted beside a concrete pillar in the arena. Gold serif sprite digits show live values; SDK serif lettering labels them. At 30 seconds of earned airtime, a 0.55-second wind-flash plays; each 60 seconds, a looping 2.4-second golden vortex plays in the streak banner. Every 100 seconds through 1,000 seconds, a generated award frame briefly flashes at center and animates into the top streak slot; the highest frame repeats for later hundreds. Paused time does not advance effects, and replay rearms the attempt milestones. HUD animations and cached updates are capped at 20 Hz. The ten transparent 640 × 214 milestone frames add about 2 MB compressed. See [asset list and exact generation prompts](assets/ui/modular/ASSETS.md). Browser fixture checks cover desktop and landscape phone; native client FPS is not measured.
- Client detection is optional and defaults to weaker mobile lift when unavailable. Tap Lift: Mobile/Desktop to correct a misidentified client or compare tuning.
- Up to **twelve golden recovery orbs** spawn around flight paths in height lanes 3.5, 7, 10.5 and 15 m, at radii 9, 13 and 17 m. Their smaller, semi-transparent gold bodies use a stronger pulsing emissive swirl with a camera-facing spinning `vortex.png` overlay; the former decorative highlight sphere is removed. Orbs orbit and bob gently. Spawns avoid the player, other orbs and leaf platforms.
- Contact with the local avatar pops an orb, including while the timer is paused/finished or the desktop cursor is unlocked. The contact radius is 1.25 m and uses the avatar's approximate vertical capsule and relative motion between frames, so fast passes and upper/lower body touches register. Teleport-sized position changes and invalid frame gaps are not swept through intervening pickups.
- The HUD teleport-orb counter shows progress from 0/10 to 10/10 and sits compactly on the right. The bottom-center gauge shows only current altitude and one continuous fill bar.
- A pop requests a small upward impulse: **1.2 desktop / 0.7 mobile**, reduced as sampled upward speed approaches 3 m/s. At or above that sampled speed the orb still visibly pops, with no added lift. Pickups share a **0.8-second local lift cooldown**: additional contacts still pop, but cannot stack impulses. Each tenth local orb pop teleports that player instantly to the scene center (**x/z 24, y 30 m**). A 12-second return window gives room to fall back through the arena; the expected teleport displacement is excluded from interruption checks so airtime resumes with observed movement. This move is local-only and is not swept through bubble pickups. The threshold, destination height and return window are in `src/orb-reward.ts`. Orbs never award time or points directly.
- Popped orbs and their vortex overlay scale down and fade over 0.28 seconds while eight golden sparks burst from the pickup point. The fixed sixteen-spark pool is reused; expired sparks hide after 0.55 seconds. Orbs respawn after 5 seconds; untouched orbs relocate after 24 seconds. Each player has their own orb pool and pickup cooldown. There are no orb colliders, messages or network updates. All effect entities are pooled, with no wind planes restored.
- Pop audio reuses `assets/sounds/hit3.mp3` on one local audio source, with a 0.12-second sound interval; repeated playback restarts that source instead of layering sounds. **FX 50%** cycles through mute, 25% and 50%. The visible pop remains readable when muted.

The two lift areas are mathematical regions, not solid boxes. Each has one force source; editor wind scripts remain detached to avoid duplicate forces. The rejected containment box and green launch deck are not created. Old `world.ts`, `course.ts` and `ui.tsx` remain dormant. Earned airtime medals remain saved; this compact HUD displays airtime, personal best and standings.

## Launch

Use Node 24 or later. From this directory, start the existing records service in one terminal:

```powershell
node server/index.js
```

In another terminal:

```powershell
node node_modules/@dcl/sdk-commands/dist/index.js build --production --skip-install
node node_modules/@dcl/sdk-commands/dist/index.js start --skip-build
```

Or use `npm run build -- --production --skip-install`, then `npm start -- --skip-build`.

**Stop and relaunch an existing preview, and reload the project in Creator Hub** to load the new nine-parcel layout and scaled model. Both `scene.json` and the editor composite now have matching geometry, ground spawn and enabled portable experiences; stale launch-deck metadata was removed. Spawn should be at floor level near the new center.

1. Verify the lift label matches the device. Jump into the central air tunnel at x/z 24, then steer and glide out of its 5.4 m square footprint. Only upward lift should occur inside; outside, there should be no wind rotation, inward pull, outer containment or broad lift. Test both Lift settings. Platforms should continue orbiting.
2. Fly above the floor: airtime increases. Land on an orbiting emerald leaf: time pauses. Test riding it, walking toward its edge, and jumping off at different points in its orbit. Counting should resume on departure; wind resumes only on re-entering the central volume. Check for slipping, jitter, clipping and takeoff failures in each native client.
3. Leave a leaf unused for at least a minute: it should keep orbiting without expiring. Land on it: the leaf should pulse without overhead text. Step off and back on: the countdown must continue. At two seconds it drops and releases the player. Repeat across all eighteen leaves and all three height lanes, including brief edge landings and contacts while descending. Verify the other unused leaves stay solid, replacements appear, and all phases remain opaque. Check center/arena clearance and each visible edge and underside; the GLB itself should be the collider, with no oversized invisible disk. Confirm the decorative wind planes are absent while the wind still moves you.
4. Touch the floor: the final time remains visible. Take off again: only current airtime resets. Check live runners in the mid-right HUD list; the physically mounted board beside the north-west concrete pillar shows persistent global weekly standings. Restarting the scene restores the personal best and board records while the service runs.
5. Unlock the desktop pointer or suspend the client: inactive time must not count. Leave and re-enter the arena while airborne: the run should pause outside and resume inside without crediting the gap. Land to retry after a disconnect, unexpected teleport or long sample gap.
6. Repeat with two identities on desktop and mobile. Record device names, client versions, FPS, landing/takeoff behavior and control problems.
7. Fly through a spinning golden orb while descending: verify the swirl overlay and stronger glow animate, the smaller translucent body and sprite scale down and fade, golden sparks emit from its origin, and it eventually respawns. Check motion at 30/60 fps and the expanded 1.25 m contact radius during fast passes, including head/feet contact. Repeat with the timer paused or finished and cursor unlocked: contact should still pop. While rising quickly, the boost should be reduced or absent at the cap. Nearby contacts during cooldown should still pop without additional lift; neither the timer nor leaderboard gains seconds directly. On the tenth local pickup, verify one instant move to (24, 30, 24), the timer continues after the teleport discontinuity and during the 12-second return window, and ten further pops are required before the next move. Mute FX and repeat. Check that another player's pickups do not remove your orbs or move your avatar.

For a phone or another computer, `127.0.0.1` points to that device. Set `TUNING.persistence.previewUrl` in `src/tuning.ts` to a reachable records URL and rebuild. For LAN testing, set `$env:HOST='0.0.0.0'` before `node server/index.js` and use the PC's LAN address on port 8787. Published scenes use `publicUrl`, which needs an accessible HTTPS service. No deployment or firewall changes are performed automatically.

Persistence reuses the existing HTTP service and `server/vortex-v1.json`. Without a service/configuration, the physical board reports offline/session-only records. The HUD list contains only players currently present in the scene. Local airtime is exact; remote airtime is estimated from observed avatar movement because the SDK does not expose another player's local platform support/timer state. Persistent global standings are client reported and unauthenticated, suitable for playtesting rather than verified competition.

## Checks

For an isolated visual layout check, run `node scripts/preview-hud.cjs` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8127`. It renders the actual HUD component tree in a browser fixture with desktop, landscape-phone, long-number and streak examples; portrait intentionally shows no HUD. This does not simulate native Decentraland rendering or measure device FPS. Visual inspection covers 1280×720 and 844×390 layouts. Buttons and labels retain an 11-pixel minimum; long numeric values shrink within their panels. The browser fixture does not measure native device FPS.

```powershell
node tests/run.cjs
node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit
```

The existing automated checks cover airtime transitions, frame-rate independence, interruption and independent player state; active vortex rotation, lift boundaries and force replacement; preserved glider settings with no wind planes; matching 3x3 scene/editor metadata; pooled leaf limits, GLB mesh collision, bounded orbital wobble, separation, bounds, support and suspension cleanup; looping music startup; unused-leaf persistence, two-second landing-triggered countdowns, repeated-contact protection, falling visuals and independent collapse; orb spin, swept capsule contact, respawn, obstacle exclusion, mute, small capped impulses, rapid-pickup cooldown and independent player state; and real HTTP persistence, ties and reset boundaries. These tests have not been extended to cover the 10-orb teleport reward. The fixtures inspect SDK ECS behavior; they do not simulate native collision physics, prove rendered smoothness on a device, or prove that a client carries the rider.

The earlier Codex in-app browser smoke test remained at Decentraland's 0% loading screen. It has not been rerun for this 3x3 iteration; current rendering and gameplay require a native-client check.

Native desktop/mobile movement feel, moving-platform riding/takeoff, touch UI, multiplayer appearance and FPS remain unverified on physical devices for this iteration. If a client slips or fails to carry a rider, test a lower `orbitDegrees` first; a stationary collision surface with orbiting visuals would change the requested behavior and is not silently substituted here. Local platforms can make another runner appear to stand on an invisible surface; test with two players before expanding. Different lift settings do not establish device fairness. Mobile suspension protection depends on client pause/connection signals: an unreported background simulation cannot be reliably detected. Ten-player capacity is unverified.

Support detection combines native local-player trigger contacts, a downward physics ray and platform geometry with an approximate 0.88 m chest-to-foot offset. See the official [player-position documentation](https://docs.decentraland.org/creator/scenes-sdk7/interactivity/user-data) and [raycasting API](https://docs.decentraland.org/creator/scenes-sdk7/interactivity/raycasting). Confirm contact tolerances in each target client.
